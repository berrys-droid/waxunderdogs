How it works

Three steps. One song that sounds like them.

No studio, no session players, no six-month wait. You bring the story; we do the rest.

01

Tell us about them

Fill a short brief — names, the moments, the inside jokes, the mood. Add photos, or a voicemail and voice memos if you have them.

02

We write & produce it

We write the lyrics from your story and produce the track in the genre you pick. Real voice clips get cleaned up and woven in.

03

You get a song to keep

A finished, mastered song, a printable lyric sheet, and a private link. Revisions included. Add a QR play-card or a streaming release.
